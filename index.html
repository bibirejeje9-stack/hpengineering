COMMON QUESTIONS

Working with HP Engineering

Can you support projects outside California?

Technical drafting and coordination may be considered across the United States when permitted by applicable professional rules. Regulated engineering, responsible charge and sealing depend on the state and the authorized professionals involved.

What should a contractor share to start?

Provide the project location, existing plans or photos if available, and a short description of the technical support needed. A preliminary conversation helps define a suitable scope.

Can you work alongside our existing architect or engineer?

Defined drafting and coordination tasks can be undertaken in collaboration with the project's existing design professionals. Responsibility for regulated engineering remains with appropriately licensed professionals.
